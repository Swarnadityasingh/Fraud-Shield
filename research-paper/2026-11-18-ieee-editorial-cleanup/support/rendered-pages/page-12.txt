C. Construct validity and gap assessment 
Imbalance is partially addressed , not solved: the study 
compares weighting and thresholds, yet both selected models 
miss substantial positive cases. Explainability is also partially 
addressed only at the global descriptive level: impurity ranks 
are present, while SHAP, local explanations, counterfactuals 
and their fidelity/stability are not demonstrated. [E04, E08–E10] 
Unified application architecture is implemented, but unified 
predictive modeling is absent. Two scores without demonstrated 
calibration and with different targets cannot be treated as a 
validated joint financial-risk score merely because they share a 
page. Temporal fraud evaluation is present, whereas concept-
drift detection and adaptation are not implemented or 
experimentally evaluated . The selection reversal is an 
observation, not a drift diagnosis. [E02, E04, E07–E09] [6] 
Calibration is unmeasured. Neither displaying percentages 
nor using a probability -producing estimator validates absolute 
risk. Conversely, external calibration findings do not prove these 
models are miscalibrated. The UI bands are conventions without 
empirical risk-band validation, and F1 does not encode actual 
monetary costs. [E04] [3], [12], [13], [14] 
D. Reproducibility, operations and publication boundaries 
The preserved package includes sources, scripts, artifacts, 
metrics, figures and checksums. This improves traceability but 
is not independent reproduction. The current environment 
snapshot must remain separate from an unrecorded historical 
environment, and  hardware requirements cannot be inferred 
from a successful past run. [E14, E18, E19] 
Operational qualifications include history rows lacking 
model version/threshold attribution and the prior audit's finding 
that a downgrade through the authentication migration removes 
history ownership. Browser report/ download/print verification 
remains pending. Dataset -specific redistribution permissions, 
exact credit dictionary wording and official-release identity are 
unresolved. The manuscript is therefore an evidence -bounded 
content draft, not a claim of operational or publication readiness.

XI. CONCLUSION 
FraudShield's preserved experiments describe two 
independent Random Forest pipelines selected using validation 
F1 and evaluated on distinct internal holdouts. Both selected 
forests exceed the prior baseline in saved F1 and AP, but 
positive-case errors rema in substantial. The fraud comparison 
demonstrates a validation -to-holdout ordering reversal: the 
selected unweighted forest does not retain its F1 advantage over 
the weighted reference. The paper retains that result and its 
unresolved cause. 
The empirical contribution is the task-specific comparison of 
weighting candidates at validation -selected operating points, 
including a preserved unfavorable selection -order reversal. 
Global rankings and source/ artifact traceability support that 
interpretation without becoming claims of a new classifier or 
explanation method. Application integration is documented at 
source level and through identified prior checks; live 
verification remains prospective under O7c/ PSQ1, outside the 
answered RQ1–RQ3. Histori cal fit provenance, calibration, 
uncertainty and publication permissions retain their stated 
limits. No universal superiority, acceptance or deployment 
readiness is asserted. [E01–E11, E15–E20]

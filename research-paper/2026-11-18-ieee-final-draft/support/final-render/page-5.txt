Authentication implements JWT access, scrypt password

The backend loads selected pipelines at startup, obtains 
positive-class scores and applies the stored task threshold. 
Requests require schema-matching feature keys and appropriate 
values. Successful authenticated predictions commit an owned 
history row bef ore returning a saved ID. List and individual -
history queries filter by ownership. The database engine is 
explicitly configured; there is no exception -driven SQLite 
fallback. The health response reports model availability, not 
database connectivity or predictive reliability. [E04, E06] 
Prediction history stores model type, class, score, display 
risk and time, but not model hash or decision threshold. Owner 
IDs can be null for legacy/ detached history; such rows are 
excluded from personal history. The Unified Risk view renders 
combined hi story summaries using the same component as 
Analytics. No third inference model, fused probability or 
validated joint target is implemented. [E02–E06]

IX. DISCUSSION AND THREATS TO VALIDITY 
A. Internal validity and historical provenance 
The current scripts specify training -only preprocessing and 
validation-based selection, while later checks confirm current 
chronological partition structure and matching stored 
preprocessing. However, source inspection is not proof that 
every historical ex ecution followed the same data boundaries. 
Exact historical weighted -fraud training -row identity remains 
unproven, as does the full chronology of analyst decisions 
concerning holdout information. These qualifications limit a 
claim of a fully matched contro lled refit or independently 
certified leakage-free historical experiment. [E08, E15] 
Credit membership reconstruction follows the preserved 
algorithm and matches saved counts, but no earlier ID manifest 
exists for direct comparison. File hashes establish continuity, not 
official-release authenticity, and equal model dimensions do not 
prove training-label identity. Missing fit logs cannot be repaired 
by asserting stronger prose. [E13–E16] 
B. Statistical and external validity 
The comparison evaluates one forest family, two weighting 
settings and one research seed, without confidence intervals, 
repeated-seed stability or significance testing. It does not 
establish superiority over logistic regression, boosting, neural 
models or other financial systems. External papers use different 
splits, feature construction, tuning and refit policies; their 
reported scores are not a matched leaderboard. [E08, E09] [5],

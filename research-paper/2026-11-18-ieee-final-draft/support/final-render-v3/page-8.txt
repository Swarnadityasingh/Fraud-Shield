The weighted comparator achieves higher holdout precision 
with similar recall. This does not justify retrospectively 
replacing the validation-selected model in the results. Nor does 
the record establish that concept drift caused the reversal. 
Distribution change, sample variation and other explanations 
require additional analysis. RQ1 is answered descriptively for 
the saved comparison, not by a causal explanation or a general 
claim that one weighting strategy dominates. [E09, E15] 
Credit's unweighted model has higher saved validation F1 
and higher holdout F1 than its weighted comparator. The 
recorded random split supports that observation only. With one 
research seed and no uncertainty/significance estimates, it does 
not establish population-level superiority of unweighted forests. 
The two datasets should not be pooled to produce a single 
financial-performance score. [E09]

## 3. Critical literature changes and limits

- Han and Jung [7]: cross-validation, resampling and explanation study is contrasted with the fixed local holdout. The available extraction does not fully audit nesting; no leakage allegation or matched-score inference is made.
- Soylu et al. [8]: different split fractions, F2 objective, multiple seeds and development refitting are analyzed as distinct methodological choices. Their refit is not declared invalid; its operating-point/fit relationship is not assumed identical to FraudShield's.
- Chen et al. [9]: sequence construction and synthetic examples change the observation/learning setup, so common source data do not establish comparability.
- Kang and Kim [10]: entity separation and interpretable additive structure ask questions not addressed by the local transaction-row split/global importance.
- Weighting/calibration sources [11]–[14]: motivate limited claims and unresolved evaluation dimensions, without diagnosing the cause of FraudShield's reversal or claiming calibration outcomes.

These changes use the existing verified literature matrix and references. They do not represent a new systematic search, full independent audit of the cited experiments or verification of unexamined implementation details.
